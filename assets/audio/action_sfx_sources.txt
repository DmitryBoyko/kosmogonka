Original procedural synthesis in tools/prepare_action_audio.py (fixed seeds).

firework: soft burst and sparkling tail. Effects use the Effects audio bus.

Existing human voice recordings and other effects are unchanged.

script, 0.49–0.68 seconds each. No human speech, downloaded samples or
